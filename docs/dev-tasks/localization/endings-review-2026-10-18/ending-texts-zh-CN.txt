舍弃凡躯，换取神格
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

世间的一切，在我看来都如此令人厌倦、陈腐、乏味，毫无意义。
How weary, stale, flat, and unprofitable seem to me all the uses of this world.

最终，我明白了埃尔西诺究竟是什么：一个装满木偶的盒子。一场演给傻子看的街头戏。
In the end, I come to understand what Elsinore truly is: a box filled with puppets. A street-show for fools.

每天，木偶们都从盒子里出来。一个新的故事开始上演。
Each day, the puppets come out of the box. A new story is told.

这个敲打那个的脑袋，这个又和那个上床。
This one beats that one over the head, this one sleeps with that one.

每讲一个新故事，就换一批木偶，让故事保持新鲜有趣，吸引观众的注意。
The puppets are swapped for each new tale to keep it fresh and interesting, to hold the crowd's attention.

……可一天结束时，所有木偶都会回到那个寂静的木盒里。一切又回到最初的起点。无从逃脱。
...But at the end of the day, all the puppets go back into that quiet, wooden box. All of it returns to the same point from which it began. And there is no escaping it.

一旦看见这个世界磨损散开的线头，我就再也无法视而不见。一旦把人们看作那些木偶，我就再也无法把他们当作完整的人。
Once I begin to see the frayed ends of the world, I can't unsee them any more. Once I see people as those puppets, I can't understand them as fully human.

我对他们所有的爱与恨都已消失，取而代之的是……一片空白。
All the love and hate I feel for them has vanished, to be replaced with... nothing.

我毫无缘由、却又有意地杀死他们，带着一种猫一般的百无聊赖。我从没想过自己心里也会有这种感觉。
I kill them senselessly, intentionally, with a catlike boredom I did not think I had within me.

就像孩子拆开一只虫子，只为看看里面有什么，我杀光了埃尔西诺的所有人，只为看看当一切伪装褪去，会发生什么。
Like a child picking apart an insect to find out what's inside it, I have cut down everyone in Elsinore to find out what happens when all pretensions fall away.

如今，昆斯给了我一个有趣得多的机会：永远合上这个木偶盒，去看看别的好戏。
Now, Quince offers me something far more interesting: the chance to close the puppet-box forever and visit other attractions.

他邀我与他同行，去看看其他世界。我欣然接受。
He asks me to come with him, to see other worlds. And I gladly accept.

想做别的选择，早就太迟了。如今知道了这一切，我就再也回不去了。
It is far too late to do otherwise. I can't go back, not after knowing what I know now.

他在空中打开了一道门，一道从未存在过的门。
He opens a doorway in the air, a door which never before existed.

我们穿过那道门，来到一个全然不同的地方，那里满是高耸的钢铁和尖啸的铁皮怪物。
We step through, and we are somewhere else entirely, a world full of towering steel and screeching iron monsters.

从那里出发，我们又去了十二个世界。他带我看到了从未有人类见过的事物。
And from there, a dozen more worlds after that. He shows me things no human has ever seen.

他平等地对待我。我面对这些新奇景象时的困惑，让他觉得无比有趣，怎么都看不够。
He treats me as his equal. He finds my confusion about these new sights endlessly fascinating.

我去过的地方，遇见的那些奇异的人与生物，我根本无从描述。
I cannot begin to describe the places I go, the strange people and creatures I meet.

但无论在哪个世界，那本书都赋予我们力量，让我们能囚禁他们，将他们拆解。
But in every world, the Book grants us the power to imprison them and pick them apart.

我见过无数血腥、非自然的死亡，由色欲、贪婪、怜悯所致。
I've seen countless bloody and unnatural ends, brought on by lust, by greed, by compassion.

我亲眼见过星辰本身造成的毁灭。我曾以为它们只是固定不动的幻象——直到我亲自去过那里。
I've witnessed destruction wrought by the very stars themselves, which I thought once were mere fixed illusion—until I'd been to them.

我的好奇心没有尽头。每过一天，我都觉得自己离理解宇宙的灵魂更近了一步。它的本性是冷漠。它的循环是死亡与重生。
My curiosity is endless. With each day, I feel I grow closer to understanding the very soul of the universe. Its nature is apathy. Its cycle is death and rebirth.

于是，我们也继续前行。
And so we, too, carry on.

我曾以为昆斯残忍至极。如今我才明白，是我对他的看法太天真了。
I once thought Quince was horribly cruel. Now I realize that the way I saw him was naïve.

我很高兴能告别过去的生活。我从不知道它竟如此可笑、简单，在宏大的布局中毫无意义。
I am glad to leave my old life behind. I had no idea how silly and simple it was, how utterly meaningless in the grand design.

我说话像个孩子，思考也像个孩子。如今，昆斯给了我机会，让我抛开那些幼稚的事。
I talked like a child, I thought like a child. Now Quince has given me the chance to put aside childish things.

就让这个男人与我一起，踏上这无尽的漫游吧。
Let us embark on this endless wandering together, this man and I.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃现实，换取欢乐
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

今天，我要去参加一场美好的宴会。
Today, I am going to a lovely party.

宴会由王后举办。最近，她待我热情又大方。
It is being hosted by the Queen. She's been warm and generous to me lately.

宴会开始前，她会来和我聊聊，我们会一起说笑，谈论今晚的安排。
She will come to mingle with me before the party, and we'll laugh together, discussing our plans for the night.

雷欧提斯如今已经回到埃尔西诺的家，不再离开了。他和父亲都会到场。他们今晚会来宴会上看我。
Laertes is home in Elsinore for good now, and he and Father will be there. They will come to see me at the party tonight.

如今他和布莉特即将成婚，我曾撞见他和父亲争论嫁妆的事，一直争到深夜。
Now that he and Brit are to be wed, I've caught him with Father debating the dowry long into the night.

今晚，父亲会把自己曾经懂得的关于爱的一切都告诉雷欧提斯：如何善待对方。如何在意见不合时仍宽容相待。如何一起成长。
Father will, tonight, tell Laertes all he once knew of love: How to be kind. How to disagree charitably. How to grow together.

昆斯和他的演员们也会到场，整晚为大家带来无尽的欢乐。
Quince and his players will be there, too, providing endless merriment as the night goes on.

哈姆雷特和克劳狄斯已经和好，成了朋友。他们今晚都会到场，宣布第一支舞开始。
Hamlet and Claudius have made up as friends. Both of them will be there tonight, where they'll announce the first dance.

哈姆雷特会向我伸出手。那只手从前也许会颤抖，但如今不会了。
Hamlet will offer me his hand. It might have trembled, once, but not any more.

当我握住他的手时，他会望着我的眼睛，对我眨一下眼，快得几乎让人错过。然后，他会把我拉向他。
And when I take it, he'll look me in the eye, and give me a wink almost brief enough to miss. Then he'll pull me towards him.

这才是「真正的」哈姆雷特。那个总爱开玩笑的人。那个我从小就认识的男人。
This is the 「real」 Hamlet. The constant joker. The man I've known all my life.

我们舞动、旋转，从霍拉旭和贝尔纳多身边经过——霍拉旭跳得轻松自如，贝尔纳多却慌张地盯着自己的脚。我忍住了笑。
As we move and spin, we pass Horatio and Bernardo—Horatio dancing with ease, Bernardo watching his feet in a panic. I stifle a giggle.

布莉特靠着墙，慢慢啜饮着一杯葡萄酒，静静地看着我们，嘴角带着一丝微笑。
Against the wall, Brit nurses a goblet of wine and watches us with a small, quiet smile.

这一晚在炉火旁接近尾声，大家尽情分享葡萄酒和奶酪，直到每个人都吃得饱饱的。
The night comes to its close by the fire, where wine and cheese are shared freely until we're all stuffed.

那晚，我带着暖意和幸福倒在床上睡去，醒来时迎接我的是阳光。
After I fall into bed that night, warm and happy, I wake to sunshine.

我穿好衣服，走向池塘。鲜花盛放。我们的夏天永不结束。
I dress and make my way to the pond. The flowers are in full bloom. Ours is an eternal summer.

冬霜永远无法触及池面，也无法夺走池边数百朵美丽鲜花的生命。
No winter's frost can ever touch the pond's surface, or kill the hundreds of beautiful flowers which line its edge.

我往水中看去，看见自己正回望着我。就在那一刻，我尖叫起来。
I peer into the water and see myself looking back at me. And at once, I shriek.

水中的奥菲莉娅憔悴不堪，双眼深陷在颅骨的眼窝里。
The Ophelia in the water is haggard; her eyes are sunken into the holes in her skull.

她的头发蓬乱不堪，像一团乌云围在脸边。
Her hair is a ragged mess, framing her face like a dark cloud.

她穿着一件白色衬裙，坐在阴暗的牢房里。她曾抓挠石头，试图逃离黑暗，指甲因此裂开，沾满了血。
She wears a white shift, and sits in a shadowy cell. Her nails are split and bloodied where she has scratched at stone, trying to escape the darkness.

水中传来低语。“……可怜的姑娘。”“彻底疯了。”“……一辈子关在这里。”“没救了。”
Whispers come from the water. '...Poor girl.' 'Gone quite mad.' '...Kept here for life.' 'Lost cause.'

渐渐地，低语越来越多，越来越响，直到那些声音冲着我尖叫，在水中轰然回荡。
Slowly, they grow, increasing in volume and number, until the voices are screaming at me, booming through the water.

水中的奥菲莉娅望着我，求我把手伸过去，将她拉进我的世界。
The Ophelia in the water looks back at me and begs me to reach through, pulling her into my world.

她向我伸出血淋淋的手。但我不能。那会打碎我们之间的墙。那会……打碎……一切。
Her bloodied hand extends to me. But I can't. That would shatter the wall between us. That would... shatter... everything.

我猛地移开视线。低语停了。头顶上，洁白无瑕的云朵在八月的蓝天中飘过。
I rip my gaze away. The whispers cease. Above me, white clouds drift, immaculate in a blue August sky.

我听见的不是人声，是蟋蟀的叫声。高高的草丛里，蟋蟀和青蛙的叫声。
I wasn't hearing voices. I was hearing crickets. Crickets and frogs in the tall grass.

一切都如它该有的样子。我不能花哪怕片刻去想另一个奥菲莉娅，那个被关在可怕地方的她。
Everything is as it should be. I mustn't spend a spare moment thinking of that other Ophelia, the one locked in that horrible place.

我同情她。但她「不是」我。
I feel for her. But she 「isn't」 me.

再说——今晚，我要去参加一场「美好的」宴会。
Besides—tonight, I am going to a 「lovely」 party.

雷欧提斯如今已经回到埃尔西诺的家，不再离开了。他和父亲都会到场。他们今晚会来宴会上看我。
Laertes is home in Elsinore for good now, and he and Father will be there. They'll come to see me at the party tonight.

如今他和布莉特即将成婚，我曾撞见他和父亲争论嫁妆的事，一直争到深夜。
Now that he and Brit are to be wed, I've caught him with Father debating the dowry long into the night.

昆斯和他的演员们也会到场，为大家带来无尽的欢乐……
Quince and his players will be there, too, providing endless merriment...

...
...

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃地位，换取家人
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

离开埃尔西诺。这是父亲最害怕的事。
Gone from Elsinore. My father's worst fear.

我们挤进仅有的一辆马车，全部家当都绑在车顶，城门在我们身后永远关闭了。
The gates closed behind us forever as we gathered into our singular carriage, all our worldly possessions strapped to the top.

我们走了两天，终于抵达丹麦边境的最外围——那里有一座默默无闻的小城堡，正在风雪与泥土中逐渐坍塌。
We traveled for two days until we reached the outer limits of Denmark's borders—a small castle of no repute, crumbling away into snow and dirt.

这片土地被赐给父亲，让我们在此安静地度过余生。
This is the land my father was given to carry out the rest of our quiet lives.

我们没有仆人，没有牲畜。只有这些破败的石墙，和我们。
We have no servants, no animals. Only these broken stone walls, and us.

起初，父亲靠着人脉，能借一点钱就借一点。但他知道我们还不起——渐渐地，这点钱也断了来源。
At first, Father uses his connections to borrow a little money where he can. But he knows we cannot pay them back—and in time, that coin dries up.

我们学着踏实干活。雷欧提斯劈柴，用来生火。
We learn to do honest work. Laertes chops the wood that feeds the fire.

我在森林里尽可能采集能找到的根茎和食物，供我们果腹，还学着设陷阱。
I gather what roots and food I can find from the forest to feed us, and learn to set traps.

有一段时间，日子过得不错，也很温暖，虽然这也算不得多大安慰。每晚，雷欧提斯拨弄着鲁特琴，父亲和我则在炉火旁渐渐睡去。
For a while, it is a good, warm life, for what little it's worth. Every night, Laertes strums his lute as Father and I drift off to sleep by the fire.

我和雷欧提斯越来越亲近。我对父亲有了从未有过的了解。他更多地谈起母亲。
I grow close to Laertes. I get to know Father in a way I never did. He talks about Mother more.

他谈起他们曾经对彼此的爱，也谈起他对我们的爱。
He talks about the love they felt for one another, and the love he feels for us.

父亲握紧我的双手，向我发誓，过不了多久，他就会带我们离开这里，去一个更好的地方。
Father clasps my hands in mine and swears to me that someday soon, he's going to get us out of here, somewhere better.

他说这话时眼角含着泪，我看得出他是真心的。
He says it with tears in the corners of his eyes, and I can tell he means it.

可是在十二月一个格外寒冷的夜晚过后，父亲清早醒来，开始咳嗽。
But one morning, after a particularly cold December night, Father wakes with a cough.

他的咳嗽一直没有好转。很快，他便卧床不起，一站起来就抖得厉害，仿佛随时会瘫倒在地。
It does not improve. Soon, he lies in bed, unable to stand without shaking so hard he looks like he might collapse altogether.

雷欧提斯和我把能找来的药和药膏都拿来给他用。我们卖掉我的几条裙子，请当地的医生来给父亲看病。
Laertes and I bring him all that we can, every medication, every ointment. We sell a few of my dresses to bring the local physician to have a look at Father.

我生平第一次祈祷。
For the first time in my life, I pray.

我不知道谁会听见，于是向圣母玛利亚祈祷。我恳求她让父亲活下去，她想要什么，我都愿意献上。只要不是他的命。什么都行。
I don't know who's listening, so I pray to Mother Mary. I beg her to keep Father alive, offer her anything she likes. Anything but his life. Anything.

似乎真的有人听见了我的祈祷。父亲的身体慢慢好转起来。
And it seems like someone is listening to my prayers. Father's health improves, slowly.

他开始能稍稍坐起来了，也开始吃东西。春天来时，我们甚至带他到外面去。
He begins to sit up, a little. He begins to eat. We even take him outside when spring comes.

他开始说，我们该离开这里，去一个更好的地方。也许往南去波希米亚，甚至去更远的地方。
He begins to talk of us leaving this place and going somewhere better. South to Bohemia, perhaps, or even further afield.

可有一天早晨，我去叫他起床时，他的身体已经冰冷。
But one morning when I go to rouse him, he is cold.

遗体僵硬，双眼凹陷，皮肤紧紧绷在骨头上。我不得不移开目光。雷欧提斯紧紧抱住我。
The body is stiff, eyes sunken, skin stretched too taut over the bones. I have to look away. Laertes holds me tight.

我们把他葬在城堡墙边一小片长着青苔的地方。我朝南望向埃尔西诺的城墙，努力不去怨恨这场流放，可是……我做不到。
We bury him in a little patch of green moss close to the castle's walls. I try not to resent our exile as I look to the south towards Elsinore's walls, and... I fail.

父亲去世后，我很难想象自己还能再快乐起来。
After Father's death, it is hard to imagine ever being happy again.

一年又一年过去，雷欧提斯和我常常几个月都只和彼此说话。让我们意外的是，我们并不怀念有别人作伴的日子。
Years pass, and Laertes and I frequently go months speaking to no one but each other. We are surprised to find we do not miss the company.

最终，我们都结了婚。雷欧提斯认识了一位娇小的女子，笑容温柔，性情文静——她是牧羊人的女儿。
Both of us marry, eventually. Laertes meets a small woman with a gentle smile and a quiet nature—a shepherd's daughter.

她的温和与他的暴躁恰好相衬，倒也和谐。
She makes a pleasant counterpoint to his stormy attitude.

而我嫁给了一位深色头发、擅长写诗的男子，他是一位男爵的儿子。
And I am married to a dark-haired man with a gift for poetry, a Baron's son.

幸运的是，他比哈姆雷特任何时候都要温厚得多。雷欧提斯把我们所有的家当都拿出来，给他作嫁妆。
Luckily, this one is far more kind than Hamlet ever was. Laertes offers him everything we have as a dowry.

我们的伴侣搬进这座破败的城堡，我们开始慢慢修缮它。如今，这里有了新的门窗。
Our spouses come to live in the crumbling castle and slowly, we begin to fix it up. There are new doors and windows now.

我们对父亲的人脉一无所知，这里便成了我们的家，今后也永远如此。
With all of Father's connections a mystery to us, this is our home now and forever.

前院勉强养着几只鸡、几头牛和几头猪。不久，孩子们也加入进来，喜欢追着它们到处跑。
A few chickens, cows, and pigs make do in the front yard, and soon they are joined by children, who like to chase them around.

这不是贵族的生活。这不是我会为自己选择的生活，也不是父亲希望我们过的生活。
This is not a noble life. This is not the life I would have chosen for myself, and it isn't the life Father would have wanted us to lead.

但到头来，我们即使算不上幸福，也已离幸福很近了。
But in the end, we are—if not happy—something very much like happiness.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃挣扎，换取解脱
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

一重苦难紧踏着另一重的脚跟，接踵而至。
One woe treads upon another's heel, so fast they follow.

一切本就注定如此。这个故事从一开始，就注定要这样收场。
This is the way things were meant to be. This is how the story was always meant to end.

那我还在为什么抗争？我已经答不上这个问题，于是把昆斯的黑色小册子交给他，走进了树林。
So what was I still fighting for? I no longer had an answer to that question, so I handed Quince his little black book and went to the woods.

来到溪边时，我的发间绕满了奇异的花环，由毛茛、荨麻、雏菊和长穗紫兰编成。
When I arrived at the creek's edge, I came with fantastic garlands of crow-flowers, nettles, daisies, and long purples crowned all around my hair.

我想让昆斯知道，我并不害怕。我不是被逼到了绝境，而是找到了答案，得胜而出。
I wanted to show Quince that I was not afraid. That this was not me being backed into a corner, but emerging, triumphant, solution in hand.

没有我，埃尔西诺的世界会更好。而我也不怕死。再也不怕了。
The world of Elsinore is better off without me. And I am not afraid to die. Not anymore.

于是我闭上眼睛，双脚先入水，纵身跳了下去。转眼间，我的裙子就湿透了，下沉得太快，已经无法回头。
So I plunged into the water, feet first, eyes closed. It was only a moment before my skirts were drenched, and I was sinking too quickly to turn back.

没有我，丹麦仍会继续前行。福丁布拉斯会入侵。王权会覆灭。所有人都会死于随之而来的大火。
Denmark will push on without me. Fortinbras will invade. The crown will fall. And all will die in the ensuing fire.

但这个世界的残壳中，会有新的事物重生。因为福丁布拉斯，那位光芒耀眼的王子，有能力统治丹麦，而我们谁也不曾真正具备这样的能力。
But out of the husk of this world, something new is reborn, because Fortinbras, that golden prince, is fit to rule Denmark in a way none of us ever really were.

福丁布拉斯领导丹麦与挪威统一，最终也将瑞典纳入其中。三国合而为一。数百年来，和平第一次降临。
Fortinbras leads the unification of Denmark, Norway, and eventually Sweden. The three countries unite together. For the first time in centuries, there is peace.

福丁布拉斯常常提起埃尔西诺的大火，那场夺走我们所有人性命的可怕灾难。他以此为警钟，将所有人团结在他的统治之下。
Fortinbras often speaks of the burning of Elsinore, that horrible catastrophe which claimed all of our lives. He uses it as a warning bell with which to rally all under his rule.

“让我们再也不要重蹈埃尔西诺的覆辙。”他这样倡议……可到头来，正是他给我的国家套上了枷锁。
'Let us never have another Elsinore,' he proposes... though he was the one who shackled my country, in the end.

难得有了这样的时候：内斗很少，也没什么需要惧怕。各国的财富不断增长。
For once, there is little infighting. There is little to fear. The wealth of all nations continues to rise.

福丁布拉斯入侵后，零星幸存的那几个人获准留在埃尔西诺，却不得再设宫廷。他们在那里默默无闻、平庸地度过了余生。
Those few straggling survivors of Fortinbras's invasion are permitted to remain at Elsinore and hold no court, where they lived out their lives in silence and mediocrity.

丹麦再也无人提起我、哈姆雷特或其他任何人——只有低声讲给孩子们听的故事里还会提及。这些孩子年纪太小，未曾亲眼见过那场入侵。
And no one within Denmark speaks of me, Hamlet, or anyone else again—save in whispers told to children too young to have witnessed the invasion firsthand.

没有人记得我们。他们把我们所有人的骨灰都倒进同一座坟墓。它位于皇家墓地最偏远的边缘。
No one remembers us. There is one grave into which they spill all our ashes. It sits at the far edge of the royal graveyard.

我们用生命换来了这份和平。我希望未来会因此更好。
We bought this peace with our lives. I hope the future is a better one because of it.

但愿如此。
I hope.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃安稳，换取冒险
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

我成了格蕾丝的船员，旧日的生活消失在她新船驶过的尾浪里。
I join Grace's crew, and my old life disappears into the wake of her new vessel.

海上的生活……与我以往的任何经历都不同。有一晚，船不停地来回摇晃，我向任何能听见的存在祈祷，求自己别被甩进海里。
Life at sea is... unlike anything I've ever experienced before. One night the ship is rolling to and fro, and I pray to anyone who's listening I won't be thrown overboard.

第二天晚上，一切风平浪静。满月照着甲板，其他船员教我打牌。我们喝着酒，直到我脑袋昏昏沉沉，蜷在甲板下的船舱里睡着。
The next night, everything is calm. The full moon shines upon the deck, and the other members of the crew teach me to play cards. We drink until my head is fuzzy and I curl up asleep belowdeck.

我很快就和船员们成了好友，也渐渐了解了他们的生活。我原以为他们脾气坏、举止粗俗，可他们热情地接纳了我，也很乐意称我为朋友。
I soon become fast friends with the crew and learn about their lives. I'd thought them crusty and crude, but they welcome me with open arms and gladly call me their friend.

格蕾丝开始教我用剑的各种诀窍。我学得出奇地快——没过多久，就连船上那些打了多年仗的男人，我也能打落他们的武器。
Grace takes up teaching me the ins and outs of using a sword. I'm a surprisingly quick study—soon I'm able to disarm even men of the crew who've fought for years.

我的力气不大，但动作很快，格蕾丝说这也是长处。
I am not strong, but I am quick, and Grace says there's value in that.

敌船的人第一次登上我们的船时，我僵住了。我险些丢了脑袋，直到一位船员喊了我一声，我才鼓起勇气，站稳脚跟迎战。
The first time we are boarded by an enemy vessel, I freeze up. I nearly lose my head until one of my crewmates calls out to me, and I am able to find the guts to stand my ground and fight.

事后，格蕾丝走过来，紧紧抱住了我。我从不知道，这样的自豪是什么滋味。
Afterward, Grace approaches me and catches me in a firm embrace. I never knew what it was to be proud like this.

我们在世界各地航行了三年，尽可能抢掠货物，再到爱尔兰近海卖掉，谁肯出钱就卖给谁。
We sail the world for three years, pillaging what cargo we might and selling it off the coast of Ireland to whomever will take it for coin.

不出海时，我们就到格蕾丝的一座海边城堡里歇息，喝酒、调情、讲故事。
When we're not sailing, we retire to one of Grace's coastal castles, and drink and flirt and tell stories.

有时，格蕾丝会向我倾诉：讲她以前的丈夫们，讲她当海盗之前的生活，也讲她对船员们的担忧。她挂念着他们，就像母亲挂念自己的孩子。
Sometimes, Grace confides in me: about her past husbands, about her life before piracy, about her fears for her crew. She worries for them like a mother over her children.

我们一起熬到深夜。我从未如此透彻地了解过任何人，而我们对彼此都是如此。直到现在，我才第一次有了回家的感觉。
We stay up long into the night together. We know each other more thoroughly than I've ever known anyone. I've never felt like I was home before now.

有一天早晨，我们身处远海，这一切突然戛然而止。那天雾很大，一艘英格兰私掠船先发现了我们，我们却还没看见它。
All of this comes to a sudden halt one morning when we are out far at sea. The day is foggy, and we are spotted by an English privateer before we see them.

我们拼命想逃开，却被它越追越近。不久，它的船员登上我们的甲板，迅速击败了我们，绑住我们的手脚，让我们在甲板上排成一列。
We do our best to flee her, but she gains on us. Not long afterward, her crew boards our deck, swiftly defeats us, ties us up hand and foot, and lines us up on the deck.

他们一个接一个割开我们的喉咙，或逼我们走上跳板落海，以此取乐。到了那天傍晚，只剩格蕾丝和我还活着——我告诉他们自己出身贵族，为我们争取了一些时间。
They slit our throats one by one, or make us walk the plank for their entertainment. By that evening, only Grace and I are left alive—I have told them of my noble blood to buy us time.

黑暗中，我们的头紧挨着。我生平第一次听见格蕾丝哭泣。我从未如此害怕过。
There in the darkness, with our heads close together, I hear Grace crying for the first time in my life. I've never been so frightened.

她一点点挪近，伸出绑在身前的双手，用指尖握住我的手。我感觉到她温热的呼吸拂在脸上。
She inches closer to me and, her hands bound in front of her, reaches out to clasp my own hand with her fingertips. I feel her hot breath on my face.

接着，她低声说了一句我从未想到会听见的话：“对不起。对不起，这场冒险没能再长一些。”
Then she whispers something I never expected to hear: 'I'm sorry. I'm sorry the adventure didn't last longer.'

我们就这样默默躺着，手指相触，直到天亮，敌人来带走我们。他们决定也割开我们的喉咙。我看着他们割开格蕾丝的喉咙。
The both of us lie there in silence, our fingers touching, until dawn breaks and our enemies come for us. They decide to slit our throats, too. I watch as they cut Grace's throat.

幸好，我只听了片刻她临死时的声音，他们就对我下了手，我也离开了人世。
Thankfully, I only have to listen to the sounds of her death for a moment before their hands are on me, and I, too, am gone.

这一生很好。和格蕾丝一样，我也希望它能再长一点。可谁也不知道，自己在人世间还剩多少时间。
This life was a good one. Like Grace, I wish it could have lasted a little longer. But none of us know what time we have left on this earth.

我永远不会知道，留在家乡的亲人后来怎么样了。他们是否幸福。是否怨我离开。我相信他们是怨过的，至少一开始是。
I will never know what became of my family at home. Whether they were happy. Whether they resented me for leaving. I'm sure they did, at least at first.

但我做出了自己的选择。而这整整三年，都让我觉得值得。每一天都值得。
But I made my choice. And it was worth all three years. Every single day of it.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃厮守，换取激情
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

我跟着奥赛罗出了镇。他带我去找他的马，马拴在通往大路前的最后一个饮水槽旁。
I follow Othello out of town. He leads me to his horse, tied up at the last trough before the open road.

他给我一些做工粗糙的灰褐色衣服，还有一块遮住头发和脸的布。我们都知道，我不能被人看见。我的模样和镇上其他人都不一样。
He gives me dun, rough-made clothes and a cloth to cover my hair and face. We both know I cannot be seen. I don't look like anyone else in town.

等其他人发现我不见了，就会派出搜寻队，沿着我们的踪迹追来。
When the others realize I'm gone, scouting parties will be sent to follow in our tracks.

但到了丹麦边境附近，他们就会放弃——而我们日夜兼程，比他们快得多。
But they will give up around Denmark's border—and we are far swifter, traveling day and night alike.

奥赛罗打算去威尼斯。那是一座庞大的贸易城市，我们很容易藏身其中。于是我们继续向南，我见到了这辈子从未见过的地方。
Othello has a mind to head to Venice. As a massive trade city, it'll be simple for us to hide there. So we continue south; and I see lands I've never seen in my life.

我做梦也没想过，世上还有这样的地方。
I never dreamed such places existed.

路上，我们遇见了一些旅人。有时他们威胁我们，有时对我们不理不睬；但他们也常常邀请我们围坐在火堆旁，一起吃饭。
On the path we encounter travelers. Sometimes they threaten us, other times they ignore us; but frequently they offer to break bread with us, together around a fire.

有天晚上，我们遇到了一位远行的神父。他要去遥远北方的一座小修道院。他问我们是否已经成婚；奥赛罗和我对望一眼，不好意思地承认还没有。
One night, we cross paths with a traveling priest. He is headed to a small abbey in the far north. He asks if we are wed; Othello and I look at one another and sheepishly admit that we are not.

那天晚上，神父引导我们互相许下婚誓。他用粗绳将我们的手绑在一起，低声为我们祈祷。
That evening, the priest walks us through exchanging our vows. Binding our hands together with rough cord, he whispers a prayer over us.

我从未感觉自己如此被爱、如此被渴望。那晚入睡前，我最后看到的是他的眼睛，他的双臂紧紧环抱着我。
I have never felt so loved, or so wanted. His eyes are the last thing I see before I fall asleep that night, his arms wrapped tightly around me.

……奥赛罗，我的丈夫。
....Othello, my husband.

一个月后，我们抵达威尼斯。巨大的白色建筑笔直地延伸到水道边，船只飞快地来来往往。
A month later, we reach Venice. Enormous white buildings drop steeply off into the waterways, boats zipping to and fro.

奥赛罗和我就在这里开始了新的生活。
It is here where Othello and I begin our new life.

我写了一封信寄往埃尔西诺，收信人只有一个：霍拉旭。说到底，在那些人中，只有他能让我相信，他不会来找我。
I write a letter to Elsinore, addressed to just one person: Horatio. The only one of them I can trust not to come looking for me, in the end.

几个月后，我竟然收到了一封回信，大感意外。
Several months later, much to my surprise, I receive a reply.

“信不信由你，我从前在威尼斯还挺受欢迎的。别过得那么窘迫。让朋友们帮帮你吧。爱你的，霍。”
'I was once a popular man in Venice, believe it or not. Don't live in squalor. Let your friends help you. Love, H.'

信里附着市中心一家酒馆的地址。我们去了那里，打听霍拉旭的事；对方立刻给我们安排了酒馆阁楼上的一间小屋，温暖、干燥，也很朴素。
Attached is an address for a tavern in the center of town. We visit it and inquire about Horatio; we are instantly given a small room in the tavern's attic, warm and dry and modest.

我被安排在酒馆里干活，给那些常来这里的士兵和商人端上葡萄酒和麦芽酒。
I am put to work serving wine and ale to the many soldiers and traders who call the tavern their mainstay.

奥赛罗的志向比端酒倒酒远大，于是加入了威尼斯共和国的军队，成为一名士兵。
Othello, having grander ambitions than pouring drinks, joins the Venetian Republic as a soldier.

起初，他尽量少做些事，每晚太阳落山时就回家来陪我。
At first he does as little as he can, and comes home to me by the time the sun sets each night.

但渐渐地，他在外的时间越来越长。他很快便步步高升。指挥官们倚重他的见识。他有着少有人具备的敏锐头脑和冷静才智。
But over time, he is gone longer and longer. He rises the ranks quickly. His commanders lean on him for his insight. And he has the sharp kind of mind and level-headed intellect that few men possess.

……有一天，他比平时更晚还没回来，我便跑到街上去找他。
...One day, he is gone later than usual, and I run out into the streets to find him.

没走多远，我就看见他站在大理石广场中央，朝上方一位俯身探出阳台的女子喊话。苔丝狄蒙娜。
I don't have to go far. I see him standing in the middle of a marble square, calling up to a woman who leans over a balcony above him. Desdemona.

她和我想象中一样美丽。她的眼睛明亮，渐暗的夕阳透过她，令她光彩照人。
She is as beautiful as I imagined she would be. Her eyes are bright, and as the fading sunlight shines through her, she is radiant.

我看见他抬头望着她的神情。我听不见他们说什么。但显然，这不是他们第一次交谈。也不会是最后一次。
I see the way he looks up at her. I cannot hear their conversation. But it is clear that this is not the first time they have spoken. And it will not be the last.

无论我怎么讲道理，怎么恳求，怎么哭泣，奥赛罗都已经见到了她……昆斯警告过我的一切，开始一一成真。
As much as I speak reasonably, as much as I beg, as much as I weep, Othello has laid eyes on her... and everything Quince warned me about begins to come true.

他一天比一天更爱她，也一天比一天更不爱我。
He loves her more each day, and me less.

可人心就是如此。前一刻，你的心意还能坚定如石。
But that's the thing about human hearts. One moment, you can be as certain as stone.

下一刻，只消一点细微的变化——那双明亮的眼睛投来一瞥——一切就都变了。
And the next, with just a slight shift—one look of those bright eyes—everything has changed.

最后，我再也承受不住。我叫他走，去和她在一起。
Eventually, I can bear it no more. I tell him to go, and to be with her.

不过，他倒也坦诚，没有骗我，也没有求我挽留。他点点头，吻我道别，便一言不发地走了。
And to his credit, he does not lie to me or beg me to keep him. He nods, kisses me goodbye, and leaves without another word.

但我独自站稳了脚跟。我不需要任何人。我在酒馆工作，渐渐喜欢上自己这简单、舒适、界限分明的生活。
But I stand alone. I need no one. I work at the tavern, and grow to like my small, comfortable, well-defined life.

有时，在那些安静的时刻，我甚至渐渐爱上了这样的生活。
I even grow to love it sometimes, in the quiet moments.

那些年，我爱着一个爱我胜过生命的男人。那份爱超越星辰。那份爱甚至让苔丝狄蒙娜也黯然失色。
For years I loved a man who loved me more than life itself. A love to surpass the stars. A love which outshone even Desdemona.

我永远不会忘记那份爱。但我该继续往前走了。而我也做到了。
I will never forget that love. But it's time for me to move on. And I do.

威尼斯是一座神奇的城市，一座有呼吸、有生命的城市。这里处处是艺术、生活、上帝，还有大海的气息。
Venice is a magical city, a breathing, living city. It is full of art and life and God and the smell of the sea.

我在这里找到了属于自己的慰藉。至少这里不是埃尔西诺。至少这里不是丹麦。
I find my own comfort here. At least it's not Elsinore. At least it's not Denmark.

我永远不会知道，留在家乡的亲人后来怎么样了。他们是否幸福。是否怨我离开。我相信他们是怨过的，至少一开始是。
I will never know what became of my family at home. Whether they were happy. Whether they resented me for leaving. I'm sure they did, at least at first.

但我做出了自己的选择。而这一切都值得。每一天都值得。
But I made my choice. And it was worth it. Every single day of it.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃纯真，换取权力
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

没有其他人有资格坐上丹麦的王位。是时候让有能力的人来统治了。
No others were qualified to sit the throne of Denmark. It is time to let someone competent rule.

谁能比见过所有可能命运的女人更合适？还有谁能拥有我掌握的力量？
Who better than the woman who has seen all possible fates? Who else can have the power I hold?

我曾是卡珊德拉，身负诅咒：向人们发出警告，却始终无人肯信。
I was once Cassandra, cursed to warn people and have them reject me.

如今我是堤喀，创造自身命运的女神。
Now I am Tyche, the goddess who makes her own destiny.

福丁布拉斯在埃尔西诺的王座厅与我会面，我们第一次能够以平等的身份交谈。
Fortinbras meets me in Elsinore's throne room, and for the first time we are able to speak as peers.

他也认为这场战争毫无意义，于是我把那一小片沼泽割让给他，就此结束了战争。
He agrees the war is senseless, so I cede him his little swamp, and we end it.

给他就是了。全给他就是了。男人的自傲总会让他们走向灭亡。
Let him have it. Let him have it all. Men's pride will always be the end of them.

如今丹麦的疆域确实小了，却也更加富足——挪威转而盯上了瑞典，两国斗得两败俱伤。
Denmark is smaller now, certainly, but all the richer—as Norway turns its focus to Sweden and the two tear each other apart.

而要避开那种命运，我所需要做的，不过是倾听。
And all I had to do to avert that fate was listen.

贵族们对税收不满，对财富和权力从他们手中流失感到愤怒。于是我邀请他们来与我会面，听取他们的怨言。
The nobility are angry about taxation, about the flow of capital and power away from them. So I invite them to meet with me and I hear their grievances.

起初，他们觉得我不配统治。他们争论着该由他们之中的谁来当国王。
At first, they think me unfit to rule. They argue over who ought to be King among them.

但避免了与挪威的战争，让我多了几分优势。即使我的能力尚未得到证明，我也是女王。
But averting war with Norway is a point in my favor. Unproven though I might be, I am a Queen.

如今，我没有愤怒，没有自负，也没有嫉妒。我什么都感觉不到。
These days I am free of anger, free of ego or envy. I feel nothing at all.

我已经没什么可失去的了。一切，我早已失去过上百次。也正因如此，贵族们对我生出了敬意。
I have nothing to lose. I have lost it all a hundred times before. And in that, the nobles find respect for me.

克劳狄斯曾着手将权力从贵族手中收回，集中到丹麦王室。
Claudius had moved to centralize power within the Danish crown and away from nobility.

但如今，我建立了一套相互制衡的制度，确保各方都有同等的话语权。
But now, I establish a system of checks and balances to ensure all parties have equivalent say.

任何国王都不该拥有随心所欲地左右国家、发动战争的权力。我已经见过走上那条路会带来怎样的流血。
No king should have the power to sway a kingdom and begin a war as he pleases. I've already seen the kind of bloodshed which lies down that route.

我赢得了贵族们极大的尊重，他们也尽可能努力与我合作。
Having earned their great respect, the nobility endeavor to work with me as much as possible.

我们得以推行惊人的变革。在克劳狄斯独自统治时，我从未想过这些事能成为现实。
We are able to enact incredible change. Things I never imagined possible when Claudius ruled alone.

偶尔，我会听见流言和辱骂。人们觉得我冷漠。他们低声议论着宫廷里接连发生的死亡，那些让我成为女王的死亡。
Now and then, I hear rumors and insults. People think me cold. They whisper of the deaths which plagued our court, the deaths which made me queen.

他们认为我应该表现得更悲痛。他们怀疑，像我这种身份的女人，究竟该不该被立为女王。
They believe I ought to grieve more than I do. They wonder if a woman of my standing should ever have been made queen.

他们想知道，奥菲莉娅究竟怎么了——他们问的是那个姑娘，而非这位女王。
They wonder what happened to Ophelia, the girl—not Ophelia, the queen.

我知道那个姑娘变成了什么样。她的未来被夺走了。她被迫承受地狱般的折磨。她熬了过来，还活着，却已不再是一个完整的人。
I know what became of the girl. Her future was stolen from her. She was forced to endure hell. And she came through it, alive but only half human.

渐渐地，那些我曾称为朋友的人疏远了我。他们无法理解我如今所处的世界，我也不要求他们理解。
Over time, those whom I called friends pull away from me. They cannot relate to the world I live in now, and I don't ask them to.

我必须做对国家最有利的事。哪怕这意味着抛下过去的生活。哪怕这意味着独自承受这一切。
I must do what is best for my country. Even if that means leaving my old life behind. Even if that means I weather this alone.

城堡后方，北面的小山丘上排列着短短一排新立的白色坟墓。
Out behind the castle, a tiny row of new white graves line the small hillock to the north.

岁月流逝，它们也长满青苔，沉入泥土，与其他坟墓一样。而那些埋葬在下面的人，也渐渐淡出人们的记忆，湮没无闻。
As the years pass, they, too, grow mossy and sink into the earth, joining the others. And memories of those who are buried beneath them fade into obscurity.

我不去探望他们。我不想回忆。
I do not go to visit them. I do not want to remember.

我不想碰见那些还在的人。我不想让他们的面容在脑海中挥之不去。
I do not want to run into those who remain. I do not want their faces to linger.

生者与死者一同收进一个小小的盒子，被我埋在心底某个深处：那是装着昔日埃尔西诺的盒子。
Living and dead comingle into a tiny box which I bury somewhere deep in my heart: The box of the Elsinore that was.

从今往后，我的人生将专注于「未来」的丹麦。一个其他所有国家都渴望效仿的伟大国度。
From now on, my life will be focused on the Denmark that will 「be.」 The great nation to which all others will aspire.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃和平，换取生存
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

我向军事议会示警，告诉他们埃尔西诺的城门无人设防，我们得以及时集结，组织防御。
I alert the war council to Elsinore's undefended gates, and we are able to rally in time to mount a defense.

福丁布拉斯没能在前往大厅的路上焚烧城堡周围的镇子，迎接他的，是一队队严阵以待、兵器出鞘的士兵。
Rather than burning castle town on his way to the Great Hall, Fortinbras finds cohorts of men standing at the ready, weapons drawn.

当他被扔进埃尔西诺地牢的最深处后，尽管使尽浑身解数，也没能再逃出去。
And despite his best efforts, when he is thrown deep into the underbelly of Elsinore's dungeons, he does not escape again.

我们丹麦人虽然生性安静，但一旦被逼入绝境，总会奋起保卫自己的国家，勇猛无比。
For all our quiet nature, when backed into a corner, Danes have always risen to defend our country with unrivaled ferocity.

我们成功挡住了挪威。但这并非一劳永逸。福丁布拉斯必须被送回他的人民身边，而我们也确实放他回去了——当然，先收了赎金。
Norway is deflected successfully. But not forever. Fortinbras must be returned to his people, and returned he is—for a price.

但很快，挪威的失利让瑞典壮了胆，他们又向我们发起进攻。
But soon Sweden, emboldened by Norway's falter, moves in to attack us anew.

克劳狄斯利用即将到来的战争，扭转自己在丹麦贵族眼中的形象，并向他们征调军队。
Claudius uses an impending war to turn his image around with the Danish nobility and summon troops from them.

所以，当三国倾尽全力交战时，我们的兵力旗鼓相当。通往埃尔西诺城墙、遍布盐渍的悬崖上，战火肆虐。
So when all three countries clash in full force, our masses are equal. Across the salted cliffs leading up to Elsinore's walls, war rages.

这场无休止的战争耗尽了各国的一切，直到我们被迫四处举债，能从哪里借就从哪里借。
And that fight, unceasing, consumes everything each country had, until we are forced to assume debts from anywhere we can.

父亲、雷欧提斯和我安顿下来，度过了几个漫长的冬天。我们看着丹麦国库里的黄金一天天耗尽。
Father, Laertes and I settle in for several long winters. We watch as Denmark's coffers are drained of gold, day by day.

最后，战争成了我们各自艰难生活的背景。
Eventually, the war becomes a backdrop to our own personal hardships.

城堡里曾经的那些奢侈品一夜之间消失了。
Things which had once been luxuries around the castle disappear overnight.

哈姆雷特与布莉特成婚，婚礼隆重，庆祝盛大。这一切，都是为了从布罗肯胡斯家族手中换来更多士兵、更多马匹、更多钱币。
Hamlet is wed, with pomp and celebration, to Brit. All this for the price of more men, more horses, more coins from the pockets of the House of Brockenhuus.

曾经丰盛的宴席，变成了只有炖菜和面包的简单晚餐。
Once-lavish feasts become a simple dinner of stew and bread.

我们只在内部低调庆祝节日，不再举办乔特鲁德王后从前喜爱的那种奢华盛会。
We celebrate festivals quietly, internally, and do not throw the extravagant galas once common to Queen Gertrude's tastes.

父亲无计可施，便让人卖掉我最好的礼服，为我置办嫁妆，然后提出将我许配给一个富裕人家的儿子，一个我从未见过的男人。
At his wit's end, Father has my finest gowns sold to pay for a dowry for me, then offers my hand in marriage to the son of a wealthy family, a man whom I have never met.

但一周后，我的未婚夫便死在了前线。一桩带着黑色幽默的幸事。
But my husband-to-be is killed a week later on the war front. A dark-humored blessing.

克劳狄斯竭尽所能地向国民征税。他们挨饿。我们活着。
Claudius taxes his countrymen to the greatest extent possible. They starve. We live.

「我们活着。」这才是最要紧的，不是吗？「我们活着。」我们作为同一座城堡里的人，作为一个大家庭，一起等待余生过去。
「We live.」 That's what's most critical, isn't it? 「We live.」 We wait out the rest of our lives, together as one castle, as one extended family.

所有人都活了下来。福丁布拉斯第一次进攻时，我就知道，这就是我想看到的未来。
Everyone survives. The first time Fortinbras attacked, I knew that was the future I wanted to see.

靠着自己的努力，我让它成为了现实。我拥有那样的力量。
Through my own labor I was able to make it come true. I had that power.

完好无损地活下来，哪怕不快乐，也远比死在彼此手上要好，对吧？
It is far better to have survived, intact and unhappy, than to have died at each others' hands, right?

只要我们这几个人还活着……只要我这一小群人还活着……我们就能从彼此身上找到些许慰藉。
As long as we few are still alive... as long as my small tribe still lives... we can find some comfort in one another.

这样的生活谈不上荣耀。随着战争的阴影渐渐退去，丹麦也越来越默默无闻。
This life is not glorious. Denmark slips more and more into obscurity as the shadow of war shrinks away.

我们是个贫穷的国家。在我的有生之年，我们都会是个贫穷的国家。
We are a poor nation. As long as I live, we will always be a poor nation.

那就让它渐渐淡去吧，留在蒙尘的书页和模糊的记忆里。
Let it slip away then, into dusty books and faint memories.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃永恒，换取复仇
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

我不会做任何选择。我会把昆斯留在这里，和我一起困在这地狱里。永远。
I will make no choice. I will keep Quince here in this hell with me. Forever.

我把书扔进大厅的火里。它烧了起来，却什么也没发生。
I cast the book into the fire of the Great Hall. It burns, but nothing comes of it.

当福丁布拉斯的士兵向埃尔西诺推进，时间重置时，那本书就在那里，在我卧室的桌上。
When Fortinbras' men advance on Elsinore and time resets, the book is there, on my bedroom table.

随后，我把书紧紧抱在胸前，躺在床上，一连躺了几周。每隔四天，城堡便被烧成灰烬。每隔四天，我便再次醒来。
Then, I clutch the book to my chest for weeks and lie in bed. Every four days, the castle burns to the ground. Every four days, I wake up again.

起初，昆斯会走进我的房间威胁我——尖叫——怒吼——使尽一切办法来吓唬我。
At first, Quince would come into my room and threaten me—scream—shout—anything to be frightening.

他会在地板上投下长长的影子，或召出幻象，让它们低声呼唤我的名字。
He would cast long shadows on the floor, or summon illusions to whisper my name.

后来，我除了盯着地板，什么都不肯做，他便开始恳求。那反而更吓人。
Then, when I refused to do anything other than stare at the floor, he began to beg. That was more frightening.

看着这样一个强大的男人沦落到泪流满面，无力地跪在地上，紧抓着我的手哭泣……
To see such a powerful man being reduced to tears, a limp figure kneeling on the floor, clutching my hand and weeping...

但即便如此，我也清楚地知道那是什么：不过是想从我这里骗取一点怜悯的伎俩。我没有怜悯。对他没有。
But even then, I knew precisely what it was: A ruse designed to extract some pity from me. I have none. Not for him.

如今，埃尔西诺对我而言，不过是一具毫无生气的空壳。我在它的厅廊间走来走去，感觉已有百年。令人厌倦，陈腐，乏味，毫无意义。
Elsinore is no more than a lifeless, empty shell to me now. I have walked its halls for what feels like a hundred years. Weary, stale, flat, and unprofitable.

我永远无法离开。而只要我拒绝使用这本书，昆斯也无法继续前行。他「需要」它。他需要它才能离开这个世界。
I can never leave. And so long as I refuse to use the book, Quince can't move on, either. He 「needs」 it. He needs it to leave this world behind.

我不会再让他制造痛苦，不会让他像伤害我一样伤害别人。
I will not allow him to cause any more pain, to hurt anyone else the way he's hurt me.

哪怕这意味着我必须承受他施加给我的痛苦，重复上千次……
Even if it means I must endure the anguish he imparts on me a thousand times...

哪怕他杀了我，把我的皮从骨头上撕下来，或把我变成肉泥……
Even if he kills me and rips my skin from my bones, or turns me into pulp...

哪怕他淹死我，或让哥哥用刀刺我，或让父亲徒手挖出我的双眼……
Even if he drowns me, or has me stabbed by my brother, or has my father tear my eyes out with his bare hands...

我也绝不会把这本书交给他。「绝不。」
I will never up give this book to him. 「Never.」

在这片让我们永远经历创造与毁灭的虚空里，他可以做我唯一的同伴。
He can be my sole companion in this void of our eternal creation and destruction.

我的同伴……也是我的囚徒。
My companion... and my prisoner.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃家人，换取基业
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

哈姆雷特和我已经正式订婚。婚礼当天，天刚亮，我便被叫醒，准备穿戴。
Hamlet and I have been formally betrothed, and as our wedding day dawns, I am awoken to be dressed.

十二名女仆来为我梳妆，带着我的礼服、丝绸鞋、一条厚厚的白色面纱，以及各种香油和用具。
A dozen maids come for me, bearing my gown, silk slippers, a thick white veil, and all manner of scented oils and tools.

她们洗净我的手、脚和身体，梳理我的头发，将鲜花编入发间。为哈姆雷特的新娘打扮，花多少钱都不能吝惜。
They cleanse my hands, my feet, my body. They brush my hair and weave flowers into it. No expense may be spared for Hamlet's bride.

我像个娃娃一样坐在那里，任由她们随意摆弄。在她们手里，我任人摆布。
I sit there like a doll, allowing them to push and pull me any way they please. I am pliant in their hands.

他们说……雷欧提斯……已经气得发疯了。
They say... Laertes... has gone quite mad with fury.

雷欧提斯和父亲被勒令离开城堡后，就走了。我不知道他们去了哪里。
After Laertes and Father were ordered out of the castle, they left. I do not know where they went.

我知道，无论如何都无法改变哈姆雷特的心意。二十年的仇恨与猜忌，不会在短短四天里消失。
I know nothing would have changed Hamlet's mind. Two decades of hatred and mistrust doesn't vanish in four short days.

在这个世上，我最爱的人就是哥哥。我告诉自己，只要他活着就够了。我告诉自己，我别无选择。
I love my brother more than anyone in this world. I tell myself that his life is enough. I tell myself I had no other choice.

我努力不去想象他身处某个寒冷孤寂的地方，恨着我。
I try not to think of him somewhere cold and lonesome, hating me.

有一次，我试着寄出一封信。只有那一次。我把信托付给一名侍女，只要她肯把我那张纸条送到镇上的驿站，我就给她整整一袋金币作为酬劳。
Once, I try to send a letter. Just once. I entrust a chambermaid, give her an entire pouch of gold for her trouble if she'll only take my scrap to the post in town.

……可始终没有回音。我说服自己，雷欧提斯读了我的信，只是没法回信。这样想，夜里会好睡些。
...But nothing ever returns. I convince myself that Laertes read my letter but was unable to reply. It's easier to sleep that way.

三个月后的一个早晨，我醒来，穿好衣服，被领到小教堂的祭坛前。
One morning three months later, I awake, dress, and am led to the chapel altar.

哈姆雷特站在那里，等着迎接我。他身着华美的红金色礼服，戴着父亲的王冠。他朝我微微鞠躬，露出微笑。
There, Hamlet stands ready to greet me, resplendent in red and gold, wearing his father's crown. He gives me a slight bow and a smile.

从他容光焕发的脸上，我看得出，他从未因见到我而如此高兴过。在他面前，贵族中最有权势的一群男人坐成一排，注视着——等待着。
I can tell from the glow on his face that he's never been so pleased to see me. Before him, a row of the most powerful men among the nobility sit watching—waiting.

他们冷眼盯着我。他们并不喜欢这桩婚事。在他们看来，这是一场确保丹麦王权稳固的交易。
They stare at me with cold eyes. They have no great love for this union. To them, it is a transaction to ensure the Danish crown remains intact.

而且，这还是一桩门不当户不对的婚事。穷公爵的女儿。算不上真正的贵族。远非他们为哈姆雷特安排婚事时，所盼望得到的丰厚财富。
And it is an uneven match, at that. The poor Duke's daughter. No true nobility. Not quite the riches they'd hoped to reap in making Hamlet's marriage.

他们会一直盯着我们。哈姆雷特还不是一位强有力的国王——至少现在还不是。
They will always be watching over us. Hamlet is not a strong King—not yet.

他们会左右他的每一步行动。我们永远不会自由。「我」永远不会自由。
They will drive his actions at every turn. We will never be free. 「I」 will never be free.

婚礼之前，乔特鲁德来找我谈过。她说了一番我永远不会忘记的话。
Before this wedding, Gertrude came to speak to me. She told me something I'll never forget.

她说……人根本无法在婚姻中找到幸福。
She said... that there is no such thing as happiness one finds in marriage.

她说，人生从头到尾都是苦难，幸福只是转瞬即逝的点缀。爱，也只能暂时缓解这些苦痛。
That this life is suffering from start to finish, punctuated by fleeting moments of bliss. Love merely stops the bleeding a while.

我想，她是对的。哈姆雷特和我面对着彼此。
She's right, I think. Hamlet and I face one another.

哈姆雷特伸出手，握住我的手。他握得很紧。意思再清楚不过：我并非孤身一人。
Hamlet reaches out and takes my hand. He squeezes tight. The message is clear: I am not alone.

我希望自己最终做出了正确的选择。我愿意相信，我选对了。
I hope I made the right choice, in the end. I want to believe I did.

我愿意相信，在我可能生活的所有世界中，这一个是最好的，也最有可能让我获得幸福。
I want to believe that this is the best possible world I could live in, with the greatest chance of happiness.

哈姆雷特掀起我脸上的面纱。我们一同转向祭坛。光从窗外倾泻而入，透过我的礼服和面纱。
Hamlet lifts the veil from my face. We both turn to the altar. Light pours through the windows, passing through my gown and veil.

我觉得自己轻飘飘的，仿佛随时都能飘走。
I feel ethereal, like I could float away.

毕竟，今天这么美，正适合举行婚礼。
After all, it's a beautiful day for a wedding.

...
...

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃众人，换取自己
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

离开埃尔西诺的那个早晨，寒冷而阴沉。我不知道自己要被带去哪里。已经有人替我安排好了马车。
The morning I depart Elsinore is cold and dreary. I do not know where I am being taken. The cart has been arranged on my behalf.

直到那天夜里，马车抵达一座边境小镇，我才明白自己要去哪里：回家。
It is only late that evening, when my carriage arrives in a small border town, that I realize where I am going: Home.

……至少，那是我们家族的家。一座小庄园，墙壁残破，几乎没有一扇完好的窗户，壁炉里也没有东西可烧。
...At least, it is my family's home. A small manor with crumbling walls, hardly an intact window, nothing to burn in the hearth.

我们家族曾经庞大的产业，如今只剩下这里。我将在这里默默度过余生，不反抗我们新的统治者。
It is all that remains of our house's once-great estate. Here I am to live out my life in silence, unopposed to our new ruler.

……就这样，我过了两年。迟迟没有离开。
...And for two years, I do. I linger.

我卖掉大部分华美的衣裙，雇人修补窗户和墙壁。
I sell most of my fine gowns and hire men to patch the windows and walls.

慢慢地，庄园里的宅邸修整好了，恢复了往日的美丽。
Slowly, the manor-home comes together. It regains its former loveliness.

可即使修缮完好，这里也不是我的家。这是父亲的家，雷欧提斯和我从未在这里生活过。
But even when intact, it is not my home. It is my father's home, the one Laertes and I never lived in.

而这里已经没有父亲留下的任何痕迹。所以，它永远也成不了我的家。
And nothing remains of Father here. So it can never be mine.

一天，一名信使来了。我得知新王后召我前去——布莉吉塔王后。
One day, a messenger arrives. I learn that the new Queen has sent for me—Queen Birgitta.

她声称只是想谈谈，可……我已经不知道该相信什么了。
She claims she only wishes to talk, but... I don't know what to believe anymore.

我见过布莉特剖开我的肚腹，让我的血洒在地上。我也见过她善良得超乎我的想象。所以……
I have seen Brit cut my gut open, spilling my blood upon the ground. And I have seen her kinder than I could ever imagine. So...

我没有应召前去，而是逃走了。向南逃。
Rather than obey her summons, I flee. To the south.

除了我唯一的母马、它的马鞍，以及一些可以变卖的珠宝，我几乎没带什么。
I take little with me save for my only mare, her saddle, and some jewelry to sell.

我不知道为什么。也许是因为我胆小。因为如今，若布莉特割开我的喉咙，我就再也没有重活一次的机会了。
I don't know why. Perhaps because I am a coward. Because now, if Brit slits my throat, I will never again have a second chance at life.

我尽可能一直往南走，最终抵达了纳瓦拉；那是母亲的故国，是她出生的地方。
I continue south as long as I can. I make it eventually to Navarre; my mother's country, the land where she was born.

在这里，在潘普洛纳，我比在丹麦时融入得好得多。
Here, in Pamplona, I blend in far better than I ever did in Denmark.

这里的男男女女，有的肤色和我一样深，有的还要更深。每一处厅堂、每一处门口，都充满歌声与舞蹈。
There are men and women as dark as me, and darker still. Song and dance fill every hall and doorway.

在这座被烈日晒透的城市待了一天，我便觉得全身都彻底放松下来。「是的，」我想。「这里才是家。」
After one day in this sunburnt city, I feel my body in full relief. 「Yes,」 I think. 「This is home.」

等珠宝都卖光了，我在城边一座华丽的庄园里找到了贴身侍女的工作。我服侍的玛格丽特夫人很严厉，但并非不讲道理。
When I run out of jewelry to sell, I find work as a lady's maid at a splendid estate on the edge of town. My lady Margaret is a stern woman, but not unreasonable.

夜里，我独自走在热闹的街上，朝每一扇窗户里张望。
At night, I walk the lively streets alone. I peek in at every window.

我随心所欲地四处闲逛。想什么时候喝酒就什么时候喝，想什么时候爱、爱谁，都由我自己决定。
I meander wherever my wants take me. I drink and I love whenever and whomever I please.

我不紧不慢，做个慢悠悠的探寻者——像哈姆雷特国王的幽灵游荡在城墙上，像尘埃化成的小精灵，从不在任何事物或任何人身上长久停驻。
I take my time, a tardy explorer—like King Hamlet's spirit wandering the walls, like a fairy-sprite made of dust, never alighting permanently on anything or anyone.

偶尔，我会想起埃尔西诺的所有人最终遭遇的可怕命运。
Now and then I think upon the horrible fates all at Elsinore came to, in the end.

我没能救下他们。我的家人。我的朋友。丹麦已经彻底变了样。
I wasn't able to save them. My family. My friends. Denmark has been thoroughly transformed.

听说他们把宫廷迁到了哥本哈根。埃尔西诺城堡如今已被封闭，沉寂无声。
I hear they've moved the court to Copenhagen. Elsinore Castle now lies dormant and shuttered.

可我逃了出来，保住了性命，这才是最要紧的……不是吗？
But I escaped with my life intact, and that's what matters... isn't it?

至少，每当我陷入对那些被我抛在身后的人的回忆，久久无法摆脱时，我都会这样告诉自己。
At least, that's what I tell myself, when caught in lingering memories of those I left behind.

这并非完美的人生。离没有艰辛的日子还「远」得很。
It is not a perfect life. It is 「far」 from free of hardship.

但幸而，它完完全全、彻彻底底地属于我。
But it is wholly, thoroughly, blessedly mine.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃责任，换取享乐
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

让他们斗个你死我活吧。「我」要出去快活了。
Let them all tear each other apart. 「I'll」 be out having fun.

起初，萝茜、吉尔达和我从一家路边酒馆晃到另一家，没有什么特定的目的地。
Rosie, Guilda and I meander from roadside tavern to tavern at first, making our way nowhere in particular.

每天都是同样的探险：这家酒馆卖什么？谁在讲有趣的故事？谁足够吸引人，值得凑近瞧瞧？
Every day is the same adventure: What is this tavern selling? Who's telling interesting stories? Who is compelling enough to warrant a closer look?

而且……我很喜欢这样的日子。我对吉尔登斯吞的了解，比在埃尔西诺时深得多。我也开始用全新的眼光看待罗森克兰兹。
And... I love it. I get to know Guildenstern better than I ever did in Elsinore. And I see Rosencrantz through new eyes, too.

每当钱花光了，她们仿佛总能像变魔术一样弄出钱来。为了挣几个钱，她们几乎「什么都肯做」。
When we run out of gold, they seem to conjure it up as though by magic. They'll do just about 「anything」 for a bit of coin.

只要能让我们继续上路，做什么都行。去哪里？我们要去哪里？无所谓。我喝醉了，甚至连我们「现在在哪里」都不在乎。
Anything to keep us on the move. To where? Where are we going? It doesn't matter. I'm drunk, and I don't even care where we 「are.」

每当昆斯那令人发狂的笑容浮现在我脑海里，我就喝酒，然后它便消失了。
Whenever I see Quince's maddening grin before me in my mind's eye, I drink, and it disappears.

每当我想到父亲的尸体被塞在楼梯底下，我就喝酒，那画面便消失了。
Whenever I think about my father's corpse stuffed under a staircase, I drink, and it's gone.

每当哈姆雷特那双深色、忧虑的眼睛如幻影般浮现在我面前，仿佛要看透我，我就又开始喝酒，喝得更久，喝得更多，直到再也记不起。
And whenever Hamlet's dark, worried eyes rise up like a vision before me, peering into me, I drink again, and longer, and deeper, until I cannot remember.

那时还是夏天。所以，当我们住不起旅店，附近围着篝火歇脚的商队也都不肯收留我们时，我们就睡在田野里。
It's still summer. So when we cannot afford a room at the inn, and none of the local fireside caravans will have us, we sleep in the fields.

在这样一个风很大的夏夜，她们两个躺着睡着了，我独自坐着，面对月亮，思索我们的处境。
On one of these blustery summer nights, as the two of them lie sleeping, I sit alone and I face the moon and think about where we are.

那天晚上，我们喝了些烈酒。我的手指阵阵发麻，脸颊还因酒意而发烫、泛红。我格外鲜明地感到自己活着。
We had something strong that evening. My fingers are tingling, my cheeks still warm and flush with it. I feel uniquely alive.

我感觉有某种伟大而遥远的力量正在俯视我，希望我获得自由，去体验那个我从未了解过的世界。
I sense that some great and distant power is looking down at me, willing me to be free, to experience the world I never knew before.

难道我不配拥有这份自由？难道这不是我争取来的？难道我受的苦还不够多吗？
Don't I deserve this freedom? Didn't I earn it? Have I not suffered enough?

...
...

可我还是觉得，腹中深处有某种阴暗的东西。
And yet, I feel something dark, deep in the pit of my stomach.

我逃不掉这份痛苦。喝酒消不掉它，走得再远也甩不掉它。它始终在我身旁。
I cannot outrun this pain. I cannot drink it away or outwalk it. It is always beside me.

偶尔，我们会在酒馆听到零星的消息。王子和国王都死了。我的父亲死了。如今统治我们的是福丁布拉斯。
Occasionally, at bars, we hear snippets of information. The Prince and King are dead. My father is dead. Fortinbras now rules us.

对于靠种地谋生的农民来说，一百英里外的王座上坐着谁，又有什么区别？
To peasants who make their living in the fields, what difference does it make who sits on a throne a hundred miles away?

秋天的某一天，我们偏离了道路，迷了路，来到一个池塘边。那天天气清冷。天空灰暗而寒冷，草已枯黄。
One day, in autumn, we are lost and off the path. We come to a pond. It is a brisk day. The sky is grey and cold, the grass a dark yellow.

停下来歇息时，我看到了水面上的倒影。我看起来一点也不「像」从前熟悉的那个奥菲莉娅。
As we stop to rest a while, I catch sight of myself in the water's surface. I look nothing 「like」 the Ophelia I knew before.

我的双眼憔悴而凹陷，身体比以往任何时候都瘦。这不是我熟悉的自己。
My eyes are gaunt and hollow; my body is thinner than it's ever been. This is not the version of myself I know.

「自由不该是这个样子，」我想。
「This is not what freedom looks like,」 I think.

我不知道该怎么告诉萝茜和吉尔达。但不知怎的，也许是因为我们结伴走了这么久，她们自然而然就明白了。
I don't know how to tell Rosie and Guilda. But somehow, we've been traveling long enough together that they simply know.

一天早晨，吉尔达从身后走过来，把一只手放在我的腰上，靠着我。“四处游荡，玩累了？”
One morning, Guilda comes up behind me and places a hand on my waist, and leans into me. 'Tired of gallivanting?'

我向她倾诉了这段时间所有的感受。那些逃不掉的痛苦。那些借酒也消不掉的悲伤。
I explain to her everything I've been feeling. The pain I can't run from. The sorrow I can't drink away.

她想了一会儿，然后脸上绽开笑容。“你知道吗，我们还有一种冒险从没试过，”她说，“就是过不冒险的日子。”
She thinks a moment, and then a grin spreads over her. 'You know, the one adventure we've never tried,' she says, 'Is living unadventurously.'

到了下一家旅店，我们没有求人讲故事，也没有掏钱买酒喝。
At the next inn we come to, we don't ask to hear a story or put down coin for a drink.

我们问的是：哪里可以安个家？
Instead we ask: Where might we set up a home?

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.

舍弃独立，换取和平
================================

这就是我选择的命运。
This is the fate I choose.

我帮忙把哈姆雷特安全地交到了福丁布拉斯手中。即使其他人丧了命，我想，哈姆雷特的灵魂在那一天找到了安宁。
I helped deliver Hamlet safely into Fortinbras' hands. Even if others perished, I think Hamlet's soul found peace that day.

哈姆雷特不懂如何独自治国。他从来就没有这方面的头脑。可是，妈的，他就是愿意试一试。
Hamlet doesn't understand how to rule on his own. He's never had the head for it. But he's damn well willing to try.

我在他身上看到了一团从未见过的火焰。它从内心点燃了他，驱使他前进。他感受到了某种「召唤」。
I see a fire in him I have never seen before. It ignites him from within, spurring him on. He has been 「called」 to something.

他们之中最阴险的人称他为“戏子国王”。他坐在那里，思量着，扮演着统治者，却很少发号施令。
The most sinister among them label him The Player King. He, who sits and deliberates and plays at ruling, but rarely gives his command.

与此同时，挪威吞并了我们。和平的代价，是我们永远效忠福丁布拉斯。不久，瑞典也和我们一样戴上了枷锁。
In the meantime, Norway consumes us. The price of peace is our undying fealty to Fortinbras. Shortly, Sweden joins us in chains.

秋天到来时，我们已经臣服于另一位君主，尽管我们的精神仍未受损。
When autumn arrives, we answer to a different crown, though we are intact in spirit.

丹麦已经不再是丹麦了。
Denmark isn't Denmark anymore.

但——我所做的——那是最好的选择，不是吗？
But—what I did—it was the best choice, was it not?

哈姆雷特的父仇得报了。哈姆雷特登上了数月前被人从他手中夺走的王位。
Hamlet's father was avenged. Hamlet rose to the throne which was stolen from him all those months ago.

有福丁布拉斯在哈姆雷特身旁监督，人民不会再像克劳狄斯统治时那样，因统治者的无能而受苦。
And with Fortinbras over Hamlet's shoulder, the people will no longer suffer beneath incompetence as they did under Claudius.

我常看见哈姆雷特和福丁布拉斯一起在花园里散步，深入交谈。福丁布拉斯在乎哈姆雷特。我看得出来。
I often see Hamlet and Fortinbras walking together in the gardens, deep in conversation. Fortinbras cares for Hamlet. I can see it.

这个冲动的入侵者，与那个被他击碎了整个世界的年轻人，竟远比我所能想象的更加相像。
They are far more alike than I could ever have imagined, this hot-headed invader and the young man whose world he shattered.

布莉特和哈姆雷特在数百人的见证下成婚。哈姆雷特站在祭坛前，双手与她的手交握，望向人群。
Brit and Hamlet are wed before a crowd of hundreds. As Hamlet stands at the altar, his hands intertwined with hers, he looks out into the crowd.

他在寻找什么，目光扫过一张张面孔。随后，他的目光找到了我，露出一个淡淡的、悲伤的微笑。
He is searching for something, scanning faces. And then his eyes find mine, and he smiles a small, sad smile.

秋去冬来。我依然每天在花园里散步。我不介意寒冷。
Autumn turns to winter. I walk the gardens daily even still. I don't mind the cold.

一天早晨，我意外碰见一名士兵，正从庭园附近结冰的池塘里往外爬。他不知道小路到哪里就结束了，脚下的冰面裂了开来。
One morning, I am surprised to come across a soldier climbing out of the frozen pond near the grounds. He did not know the path's extent, and the ice split beneath his feet.

我不假思索地脱下披风和外衣，借着自己那点体重，尽力把他往岸上拉。他的双脚终于有了着力处，踉跄着爬上了岸。
Without thinking, I strip myself of my cloak and surcoat and with my meager weight pull him as far up the bank as I can. His feet find purchase, and he stumbles up onto the shore.

平安回到大厅后，他在炉火前烘着长满老茧的手指，自称“费迪南德”，并问我的名字。
Safely back in the great hall, warming his calloused fingers before the fire, he introduces himself as 'Ferdinand' and asks my name.

但我已看见他上衣胸前的挪威王室徽记，所以不肯告诉他。他已经暖和得很了。我把他独自留在那里，转身离开。
But I have seen the sigil of the Norwegian crown on the front of his tunic, so I refuse to give it. He is plenty warm. I leave him there, alone.

可他第二天又来看我，第三天也是，接连几周，天天如此。总是在庭园里闲逛。什么样的士兵会有这么多闲暇？
Still, he comes to see me the next day, and the day after that, and every day for weeks. Always strolling around the grounds. What kind of soldier has so much free time?

他看见我坐在长椅上读书，就给我带来挪威诗集。听见我哼歌，就请我唱给他听。这个费迪南德，真会讨人欢心。
When he sees me reading on a bench, he brings me books of Norwegian poetry. When he hears me humming, he asks me to sing for him. He is a flatterer, this Ferdinand.

可我还是忍不住动心。他那份单纯的痴情让我觉得可爱。一天早晨，父亲看见我们一起散步，惊讶得愣住了。
But I cannot help myself. I find his simple infatuation endearing. One morning, my father sees us out on a stroll, and he freezes up in surprise.

“奥菲莉娅！”后来，他把我拉到一旁，说道，“你倒是告诉我，你什么时候认识了福丁布拉斯国王的「兄弟」？”
'Ophelia!' He says to me later, pulling me aside. 'When did you become acquainted with King Fortinbras's 「brother」, pray tell?'

福丁布拉斯的「兄弟？」他不只是费迪南德，而是费迪南德「王子」。以他那不摆架子的性子，隐瞒身份也不奇怪。也许他从没想过这回事。
Fortinbras's 「brother?」 Not just Ferdinand. 「Prince」 Ferdinand. That he kept it secret is not surprising given his unpretentious nature. Perhaps he never considered it.

原来，费迪南德王子来埃尔西诺，是为了协助他的兄弟推进统一。我不想「再」爱上一个政客，所以不让自己对他动心。
Prince Ferdinand, as it turns out, came to Elsinore to support his brother in the unification effort. I don't want to love 「another」 politician, so I guard my heart against him.

可冬天渐渐过去，春天到来。费迪南德每次来带我散步，我都发现自己的决心……又动摇了几分。
But slowly, winter turns to spring, and each time Ferdinand comes to take me for a walk, I find my resolve... weakening.

到了二月，他向父亲求娶我。父亲自然转头看向我。“我喜欢他，父亲。”我笑着说，“真的喜欢。”
When February comes, he asks my father for my hand in marriage. My father, of course, turns to me. 'I like him, Father,' I say, laughing. 'Truly, I do.'

福丁布拉斯不在乎自己的兄弟是否娶了地位低于他的女子。我们三国的关系牢不可破。他欣然祝福了我们。
Fortinbras cares not whether his brother marries beneath his station. The relationship between our three nations is ironclad. He gladly grants us his blessing.

鲜花盛开时，费迪南德和我在我们钟爱的庭园里成婚。我们两人向来都不怎么热衷于教会。有些人觉得这有伤风化。我们并不在意。
By the time the flowers are blooming, Ferdinand and I marry in the grounds we love so dearly. Neither of us was ever much for the church. Some find it scandalous. We don't mind.

而如今，未来就在我们眼前。费迪南德本就不会长留埃尔西诺。现在，他正准备前往卡尔马，协助瑞典融入统一后的国家。
But now the future lies before us. Ferdinand was never meant to stay in Elsinore. Now he makes preparations to go to Kalmar, to assist in Sweden's integration.

他要求父亲和哥哥留下。毕竟，他们对那个已然衰落的旧世界，仍比我忠诚得多。他们属于埃尔西诺。
He demands my father and brother stay behind. After all, they remain far more loyal that faded old world than I do. They belong in Elsinore.

费迪南德向我保证，卡尔马并没有那么不同——没错，那是另一个国家，但两地很相似。于是我们收拾好行李，在婚礼一个月后启程。
Ferdinand assures me that Kalmar is not so different—another country, yes, but very alike. And so we pack our things, and one month after our wedding day, we set forth.

我必须相信，我留下的那个世界足够坚强。与此同时，和一个对我过去的生活知之甚少的男人离开埃尔西诺，感觉……很奇怪。
I have to believe that the world I'm leaving behind is a strong one. At the same time, leaving Elsinore with a man who knows little of my old life is... strange.

我差点就向他讲起那些迷失的日子：时间一遍遍重复，而我以为自己快要疯了。
I come close to telling him of those lost days I spent, when time repeated over and over again, when I thought I was going mad.

但他不会理解。没人能理解。是时候合上这本满载恐怖的书，翻开一本新书，让书页洁白如新，尚未泛黄。
But he wouldn't understand. No one could. Time to close this book of horrors and open a new one with fresh, unyellowed pages.

我也留下了其他人。父亲。雷欧提斯。旧生活的落幕，仿佛把我劈成了两半。
And I left others behind. Father. Laertes. It feels as though the closing of my old life has cut me in two.

但该舍弃过去，培育新生了。只有忘记死者，我们才有机会伸手拥抱生命。
But it's time to abandon the old and grow the new. Only by forgetting the dead do we have a chance at reaching for life.

永别了，埃尔西诺。
Farewell, Elsinore.
